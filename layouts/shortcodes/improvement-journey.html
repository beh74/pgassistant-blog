FROM EVIDENCE TO IMPACT↻ Continuous improvement
  1. 01

    Observe

    Collect repeatable database, workload, and environment evidence.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    Use deterministic advisors to identify SQL, schema, index, configuration, and maintenance issues.

  3. 03

    Prioritize

    Rank findings by urgency, confidence, effort, and workload impact.

  4. 04

    Plan

    Turn related recommendations into ordered work packages owned by DEV, OPS, or both.

  5. 05

    Implement

    Apply each change with its dependencies, SQL guidance, and maintenance requirements in view.

  6. 06

    Collect again

    Gather fresh evidence after the changes, ready for comparison with the previous collection.

  7. 07

    Measure

    Compare collections to understand what changed and whether the workload improved.

    Evidence → Impact
↳

Back to Observe. Use fresh evidence to choose the next improvement.
